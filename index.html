Questions

Is it safe?

Every tweak is backed up, verified after writing and reversible. Nothing is changed in Defender, the firewall or anti-cheat. All changes are local.

How many FPS will I gain?

It depends on your PC. We don’t promise a number; the Premium benchmark lets you measure before and after.

How do I cancel?

Nothing renews automatically: every payment is a single PayPal payment. When Monthly or Yearly ends, the program returns to Free. In the program, Settings → Deactivate license removes the license from the PC only.

Can I use one license on two PCs?

No. A license is bound to one PC and cannot be copied to another. If you reinstall Windows or get a new PC, write to support and we will release your code.

I paid but lost my code.

Enter the transaction ID from your PayPal receipt and the email of your PayPal account.

Windows says “unknown publisher”.

The program is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns about it. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
